## Findings & risks

Hand-written after reviewing the tables above, plus a few one-off checks noted inline.

### Summary

The dataset supports the MVP for **8 of 10 versions with no gaps**: FIFA 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 22, 23 and FC 24.
In these versions every top-5 club has exactly 11 starters, exactly one GK and no repeated position code,
and all face stats and GK stats are present. Two versions have holes: **FIFA 16** and **FIFA 21**.

### What breaks or bends `docs/PLAN.md` §1–3

1. **FIFA 16: 19 of 98 clubs have 9–10 starters** (section d). The missing starter is not on the bench
   under another label. The player is **absent from the FIFA 16 snapshot altogether**. The absent players
   are late-summer 2015 transfers: Draxler (Wolfsburg), Perišić (Inter), Martial (Man United), van Dijk
   (Southampton), Son (Spurs), Raúl García (Athletic), Hernanes (Juventus) and others (section b gap table).
   Affected clubs include Man United, Spurs, Juventus, Inter and Milan.
2. **FIFA 21: Roma and Spezia have no players** (squad size 0). In FIFA 21, their players are either
   clubless in the data (`club_team_id` null, e.g. L. Pellegrini, Spinazzola, Cristante, Pedro) or
   missing (Džeko, Mkhitaryan, Veretout, G. Mancini). Their XI cannot be rebuilt from this dataset.
3. **`league_name` is stale** and gives the club's *current* league, not its league in that version.
   The pipeline must filter on `league_id` (13 EPL, 53 La Liga, 31 Serie A, 19 Bundesliga, 16 Ligue 1).
   With that filter, every version has the correct club list (spot-checked: FIFA 15 EPL = the 2014-15 PL).
   The "~98 clubs per version" assumption holds, except FC 24 has 96 (Ligue 1 had 18 clubs in 2023-24).
4. **No `player_face_url` / `club_logo_url` columns** exist in this export (section h). URLs must be derived
   from ids (`https://cdn.sofifa.net/players/231/747/24_120.png`, `https://cdn.sofifa.net/teams/243/120.png`).
   A `curl` probe of these URLs (30.09.2026) returned **HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare "Attention Required" page**,
   including with a browser user-agent. Hotlinking from a real browser `<img>` was **not tested**.
   Open one of the URLs above in a browser to check. If it fails, D6's fallback (initials + flag) becomes
   the main path, not an edge case.
5. **The launch XI reflects real-world injuries at snapshot time.** In FC 24, De Bruyne, Courtois,
   Vinícius Jr. and Neuer are SUB. Real Madrid starts Kepa and Joselu, and Bayern starts Ulreich (74)
   in goal. This matches D2 (the "in-game XI"), but players may see it as a data error.
6. **FIFA 19's snapshot date (2018-08-21) is ~5 weeks before launch.** Other versions are dated 18–26 Sept.
   The evidence is mixed. Mariano is still at Lyon (he moved to Real Madrid on 31 Aug 2018), but Dendoncker
   (loaned 31 Aug) is already at Wolves. The FIFA 19 launch rosters and ratings may differ slightly from FUT
   base. From memory (unverified), Mbappé 87 (FUT 88?), Courtois 90 (89?) and Neuer 90 (89?) are worth
   checking. FC 24 top ratings (Mbappé/Haaland/De Bruyne 91; Messi/Kane/Lewandowski/Benzema/Courtois 90;
   Salah/van Dijk/Alisson/Rodri/Vini 89) match what I recall of launch, and deadline-day moves
   (Gravenberch, Doku, Amrabat, João Félix) are present.
7. **Formations** (for stage 3): 47 distinct outfield position sets. The most common are 4-2-3-1
   (`LDM RDM LM RM CAM ST`) and 4-3-3 (`CDM LCM RCM LW RW ST`), then several 4-4-2, 3-5-2, 5-3-2, 3-4-3
   and 5-2-3 variants. All codes fall into 27 known values (section e). `CB`, `CM`, `CF` and `CDM` appear
   as a centre slot next to their L/R pair in back-3, midfield-3 and front-3 shapes. The 1–2 club
   signatures with 9–10 players come only from the FIFA 16 holes.
8. **Loans** work as needed. A loaned player appears once, at the club they play for, with the parent
   club in `club_loaned_from`. Loanees appear in XIs normally (e.g. FC 24: Kepa and Joselu at Real Madrid,
   Cancelo at Barcelona).
9. **Names:** `long_name` is in native script for Japanese and Korean players (110 top-5 XI rows, e.g.
   `손흥민 孙兴慜`). `short_name` is always Latin. Use `short_name` for display, or keep `long_name`
   only as secondary text.
10. **Size:** about 310 KB minified / 53 KB gzipped per version with ~15 fields, which is above the
    "~100 KB" in PLAN §4 but still small. Deriving `faceUrl` from `id` on the client saves ~20%.

### Recommendation

- Proceed with this dataset. Filter clubs by `league_id` (not `league_name`) and join players on
  (`fifa_version`, `club_team_id` = `team_id`).
- **FIFA 16:** (a) **recommended:** keep the version and fill each hole with the highest-rated SUB whose
  `player_positions` fits the missing slot (the missing code can be inferred from the pair or symmetry,
  e.g. LCM without RCM), marked `filled: true` in the JSON. Alternatives: (b) drop the 19 affected clubs,
  (c) drop FIFA 16. This needs a decision for the log.
- **FIFA 21:** exclude Roma and Spezia from that version (96 clubs). There is no reliable way to rebuild them.
- **Images:** test the derived CDN URLs in a real browser before stage 6. If they fail, design the card
  around the fallback.
- **Manual FUTBIN check (plan stage 1):** start with the FIFA 19 players in point 6 and the FIFA 19 / FC 24
  sample XIs in section j.
